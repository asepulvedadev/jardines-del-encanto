# Jardines del Encanto: project guidance

## UI work

Before designing, implementing or reviewing any project UI, read `DESIGN.md` and
`.agents/skills/jardines-ui/SKILL.md`. `DESIGN.md` is the canonical design contract;
`desing.md` only points to it. Apply landing and CRM rules to their respective surfaces.

Use professional Colombian Spanish for product UI and English for identifiers and
technical documentation. Do not invent commercial facts from the reference poster.

The requested external design sources in `docs/DESIGN-SOURCES.md` are not installed or
approved. This file does not authorize their activation or a global skill installation.
Keep design changes local unless publication or deployment is explicitly authorized.

These instructions supplement higher-priority rules; they do not expand permissions
or introduce Gentle AI orchestration, registry hooks or receipt-driven review.
